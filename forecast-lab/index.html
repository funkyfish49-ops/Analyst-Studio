The model

Wissner-Gross & Freer (2013) proposed that intelligent behavior emerges from maximizing future freedom of action: intelligence is a force F = T ∇Sτ pushing systems toward states with a richer space of accessible futures — where T is the strength (resources/urgency) and Sτ is the entropy of possible futures over horizon τ. Their Entropica software demonstrated exactly this formalism on portfolio maximization in social-networked exchanges. This lab operationalizes that expression for trading decisions.

Mathematical core

Sτ(a) = H(W) + Ew[ log(1 + ρw·|Aw(a)|) ]
H(W) — entropy over the reachable worlds (regime × terminal-return bin), from the prior estimates.
|Aw(a)| — count of viable future actions in world w after committing action a (exit at profit, hedge, add, roll, harvest…).
ρw — surviving capital fraction in world w; a blowup world collapses every future option.
∇Sτ = Sτ(enter) − Sτ(cash)   →   F = T · ∇Sτ
The causal entropic gradient compares committing to the trade against staying in cash (maximum optionality). F > 0: the trade creates more future freedom than it costs — rare, precious (cheap convexity, asymmetric catalysts). F < 0 despite positive EV: the trade pays you to lock yourself in (naked leveraged shorts, illiquid traps). T is your risk-budget temperature — with more remaining drawdown budget, the same entropy gradient exerts more force.

What the backtest validates

Walk-forward: priors re-estimated at each step from trailing returns only (no lookahead), score computed, then realized outcome read from the actual path. If F carries information, (a) the F-filtered equity curve should beat the always-in curve, (b) forward returns should rise monotonically across F quintiles, and (c) the Spearman rank information coefficient between F and realized P&L should be positive. If none of that shows, the honest conclusion is that the entropic layer adds nothing for this series/structure — the lab is built to tell you that truth, not to flatter the theory.

Limitations & honesty

Three-regime tercile priors are a deliberate simplification — they capture heteroskedasticity, not true regime dynamics. Action sets are template approximations, not a real market microstructure model. The backtest compounds at the configured capital fraction with no transaction costs, slippage, or borrow availability constraints. Treat outputs as a structured second opinion for the research agents — not as investment advice, and never trade a model you haven't tried to break.
